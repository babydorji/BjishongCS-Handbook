About the School

Bjishong Central School, located in Gasa, Bhutan, has grown from a small primary school in 1997 into a full Higher Secondary institution offering multiple curriculum streams.

🤲

Committed to the development of a positive and caring learning environment where students' uniqueness, growth and achievement are valued with foundation for lifelong learning.

Rules & Regulations

Student Code of Conduct

All students must uphold these standards in and around campus at all times.

📋 General Rules

  • 1Students must be in national dress in all public places.
  • 2Students should not write or scribble on text/library books.
  • 3Students are not allowed to drive within the school premises.
  • 4Full school uniform must be worn during school hours and functions.
  • 5Be present in all school activities: assembly, classes, games, prayers, SUPW, etc.
  • 6Show respect to all teachers, support staff, and elders at all times.
  • 7Take care of furniture, electrical fittings, and school property.
  • 8Smoking, tobacco, doma, alcohol, drugs and gambling are serious offences.
  • 9Dangerous items (chains, knives, knuckles) are strictly prohibited.
  • 10No gang formation or unauthorized meetings.
  • 11Graffiti anywhere is a serious offence.
  • 12No fancy lockets, earrings, make-ups, long nails, or nail polish.
  • 13Speak only Dzongkha or English while in school.
  • 14Minimum 90% attendance required to sit in examinations.
  • 15No mobile phones, music sets or electronic gadgets unless permitted.
  • 16Tattoos (erasable or permanent) are strictly prohibited.
  • 17Boys must maintain prescribed hair length; girls must keep hair neatly tied with red rubber bands.

🏠 Rules for Boarders

  • 1Study is compulsory for all boarders.
  • 2Not permitted to enter hostels during class hours without medical leave.
  • 3Visitors not allowed to stay in the hostel.
  • 4Turn off lights and water taps when not in use.
  • 5Do not change your allotted place in dormitory, prayer hall, dining hall or study hall.
  • 6Building fires in or outside the hostel is strictly prohibited.
  • 7Students may meet parents only on Sundays from 12:30pm–2:30pm.

🚶 Rules for Day Scholars

  • 1Must report to school before 7:50 a.m.
  • 2No loitering outside the campus.
  • 3Go home immediately after school activities.
  • 4Not allowed to entertain boarders at home.
  • 5No visiting school on weekends unless instructed.
  • 6Not allowed to bring or supply junk and controlled substances.
  • 7Day scholars must undergo frisking at school entry gate.
Policy

School Discipline Policy

A four-level disciplinary framework ensuring a peaceful and harmonious school community.

⚠️ Level 1 (D Degree) — Minor Offences

Types of Offence:

Absenteeism from school activities · Tardiness · Unauthorized visits to staff colony · Improper uniform/half kiras/half gho · Inappropriate hairstyle · Indecent language · Failure to complete work · Eve-teasing · Verbal harassment · Negative peer pressure · Graffiti · Chewing doma
📌 Sanctions: Reminder to student · Mentor-mentee conference · Class teacher-student conference · Undertaking letter · Inform parents · Record in office file
🔶 Level 2 (C Degree) — Moderate Offences

Types of Offence:

Repeating Level 1 offence · Match making · Bunking from school/class · Chewing tobacco and smoking · Gambling · Mobile phone confiscation · Exam cheating · Hooliganism
📌 Sanctions: Statement from student · 1 hour campus detention for 5 days · Fine Nu. 1000 for mobile use violation (Nu. 500 additional if repeated) · Deduct 10% marks on that paper · Inform parents · Record in office file
🔴 Level 3 (B Degree) — Serious Offences

Types of Offence:

Repeating Level 2 offence · Rebelling against student leaders · Forming gangs based on region/religion/caste · Illicit/unhealthy relationship (proven) · Drinking/supplying alcohol · Theft and robbery · Trespassing opposite gender hostel · Physical assault without weapons
📌 Sanctions: Undertaking letter · 1 hour campus detention for 10 days · Call parents for undertaking · Refer to counsellor · Record in office file
🚫 Level 4 (A Degree) — Severe Offences

Types of Offence:

Repeating Level 3 offence despite corrective measures · Gang altercation with weapons · Resisting school authority · Bullying/physical assault with weapons · Sexual molestation or misconduct · Deliberate vandalism · Supplying prohibited substances/drugs
📌 Sanctions: Immediately call parents · Facilitate relocation to another school (consultation with Dzongkhag Education Office) · If no school accepts, hand over to parents with TC · Parents to replace damaged property in vandalism cases

Mobile Phone Policy

Guidelines for responsible use and management of mobile phones within the school campus.

  • 1Students must declare and submit phones to the warden/matron on arrival. Mentors collect and hold phones of their mentees.
  • 2Mentors may allow mentees to use phones at least once a month during designated hours for educational purposes.
  • 3Phones must be returned to mentor/warden/matron immediately after allotted time.
  • 4Students must use mobile devices responsibly and avoid inappropriate content.
  • 5Phone found in possession without submission: confiscated + fine of Nu. 1,000.
  • 6Confiscated phones returned only at end of term.
  • 7Repeated offence: fine of Nu. 1,500 and phone returned only at year end. Failure to pay results in withholding of TC and Progress Report.
  • 8Students must fill a User Form signed by a teacher before obtaining a phone.
Wellbeing

Student Health & PHC Visit Protocol

Procedures ensuring the safety and timely medical care for all students.

🩹

Minor Concerns

Headaches, stomach pain, minor wounds — handled by School Health In-charge on campus with first aid.

🏥

PHC Visits

Moderate cases referred to PHC only on Thursdays after lunch, escorted by school caregiver.

🚨

Emergencies

Seriously ill students escorted immediately to PHC by Warden/Matron with school driver. Any available teacher assists if driver is absent.

🛏️

Bed Rest

Hostel bed rest only upon recommendation from health professional (HA/Sister).

🪪

PHC Cards

Visiting cards issued during morning interval. Boys from male health in-charge, girls from female health in-charge. Must be returned after visit.

📋

Special Appointments

Wound dressing or other appointments on non-Thursday days: school caregiver escorts to PHC. Students must bring CID number.

Special Programs

Special Programs

Key school programs that support holistic development and a love for learning.

🤝 Mentorship Programme

The purpose of the school mentorship program is to allow students (mentees) to build a relationship with their teachers (mentors) grounded on honesty and trust. The program focuses on individualized care to actualize innate potential.

Mentoring helps learners reflect on what they are learning, why they are learning it, and which area of development has strengthened. It provides a framework for learners to take ownership of their learning and create a roadmap to achieve their goals.

📖 DEAR Programme

Drop Everything And Read — conducted twice weekly to improve reading culture, communication skills, and reading proficiency.

Activities: Class reading competitions · Book review writing · Writing competitions · Speeches in Dzongkha and English · Reading Week in September

🎭 Driglam Namzha

Conducted every Monday. Students observe Bhutanese etiquette and cultural values in the morning assembly wearing Kabney and Rachu. Aims to preserve and transmit Bhutanese cultural identity to younger generations.

Leadership

Captain Responsibilities

Roles and duties of all student leaders in Bjishong Central School.

🏆 School Captain

  • Promote the culture of Green school/hostel
  • Facilitate leadership qualities in students
  • Act as student representative in SMB meetings
  • Take initiative in organizing school functions and celebrations
  • Act as bridge between school authority, staff, captains and students
  • Maintain vigilance in hostel; report any emergencies
  • Overall monitoring of SUPW in school and hostels
  • Promote good discipline and attendance in dining hall

🙏 Spiritual Captain

  • Take attendance after prayer
  • Maintain safety and cleanliness of the prayer hall and Choesham
  • Carry out daily offerings at the Choesham
  • Coordinate long prayers on auspicious days
  • Help conduct School Rimdro and Choeshey Layrim
  • Assist adult scout leaders in troop-related activities

💚 Well-being Captain

  • Provide health-related information to students
  • Assist students in visiting hospital when necessary
  • Promote good health and hygiene habits
  • Monitor cleaning activities in school and hostel
  • Report to warden/matron if any student is sick
  • Assist mess committee as mess captain

🎨 Aesthetic Captain

  • Encourage students to participate in activities
  • Assist captains in maintaining hostel decorum
  • Support literary and cultural programmes as per schedule
  • Assist waste management and beautification programmes

🏃 Sports & Fitness Captain

  • Oversee maintenance and safety of sports facilities
  • Assist in coordinating morning fitness program
  • Collaborate with SSI to coordinate games as per schedules
  • Foster teamwork, leadership, and sportsmanship
  • Carry out Captain on Duty (CoD) and assist Coordinator of the Week

🍽️ Mess Captain

  • Serve as bridge between students, mess committee, and administration
  • Assist mess in-charge for smooth functioning of the mess
  • Be present during daily ration issuance and distribution
  • Monitor cleanliness and hygiene in mess store, kitchen, and dining hall
  • Restrict unauthorized students from entering mess store and kitchen
  • Collect and share constructive student feedback on food quality

⚜️ Scout Leader

  • Guide young Scouts in the school
  • Conduct flag ceremony every Thursday
  • Promote school-based Scouting program
  • Ensure all Scouts wear scarf on Scout Day (Thursday)
  • Perform Scout duty as a frisker at the school gate

🚌 Day Scholar Captain

  • Ensure safety of day-scholar students
  • Coordinate and monitor day scholar screening and frisking at gate
  • Report serious problems to school authority
  • Ensure timely and safe commute
  • Plan and coordinate activities for important events

📚 Class Captains

  • Ensure cleanliness of the classroom
  • Maintain class absentee record
  • Coordinate SUPW along with the class teacher
  • Manage student discipline and inculcate good behavior
  • Take initiative in decorating class with educative materials
  • Report to class teacher on matters of student behavior
  • Ensure participation of pupils in all programmes
